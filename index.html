Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it using the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or via the Google Play Store.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings: go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on your Android device.

You can sign out of Google Play Games at any time through your device settings to stop leaderboard data collection. Your existing leaderboard scores are managed by Google and subject to Google's data policies.

Data Retention

The Application stores game progress (scores, settings, theme preferences) locally on your device using local storage. This data is not transmitted to any server and is deleted when you uninstall the Application or clear the app's data.

Leaderboard scores are stored by Google Play Games Services and are subject to Google's data retention policies. If you would like to request deletion of your data, please contact the Service Provider at the email address provided below.
