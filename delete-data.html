Number Merge

Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and your right to control your data. Number Merge does not store any personal data on external servers controlled by the developer. All game data is stored locally on your device or managed by Google's services.

Below you will find instructions to delete all data associated with Number Merge.

How to Delete Your Data

1Delete Local Game Data

Game progress, scores, theme preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device. To delete this data:

  • Go to Settings on your Android device
  • Tap Apps (or Apps & notifications)
  • Find and tap Number Merge
  • Tap Storage & cache
  • Tap Clear data and Clear cache

Alternatively, uninstalling the app will automatically remove all local data.

2Delete Google Play Games Data

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your leaderboard scores are stored by Google. To remove this data:

  • Open the Google Play Games app on your device
  • Tap your profile icon at the top
  • Tap Settings
  • Tap Delete Play Games account & data
  • Select Number Merge to delete data for this game only

This will remove your leaderboard scores and Play Games activity for Number Merge.

3Reset Advertising Data

Number Merge uses Google AdMob for advertisements. To reset or opt out of personalized advertising:

  • Go to Settings on your Android device
  • Tap Google (or Google Services)
  • Tap Ads
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

Important: Number Merge does not collect or store any personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers on any server. The developer does not have access to your personal data and therefore cannot delete data from Google's services on your behalf. The steps above allow you to directly manage and delete all data associated with the app.
